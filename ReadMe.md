# 💫 About Me:
I’m Aqib Mehmood, a backend engineer with almost 7 years of experience building backend and full-stack systems. I currently work as Technical Lead – Backend at Manafa, a Saudi fintech platform, where I lead the backend work behind borrower onboarding, financing workflows, and company compliance.<br><br>Most of my work sits where product, regulation, and engineering meet. I design and build APIs, model complex data in a multi-company architecture, and integrate with Saudi government and financial services for identity verification, status monitoring, and compliance checks. Those integrations have to be accurate, auditable, and resilient when things go wrong. Alongside that, I build the admin tooling and document management workflows that operations and compliance teams rely on every day.<br><br>My core stack is PHP and Laravel with MySQL. I’m comfortable across the full delivery path, from database design and queue-driven processing to cloud storage, Linux and Nginx environments, and front-end work, including Arabic RTL interfaces.<br><br>As a technical lead, I care about clean architecture, reliable integrations, and helping the team ship with confidence. That means turning business requirements into sound technical design, reviewing code, and keeping systems maintainable as the product grows.<br><br>I’m always happy to connect with people working in fintech, backend engineering, or building products for the Saudi and GCC market.<br><br>Short version<br><br>Technical Lead – Backend at Manafa | ~7 years in backend and full-stack development | Laravel, MySQL, API design | Building fintech platforms for onboarding, financing, and compliance in Saudi Arabia.<br><br>I kept the integration examples generic rather than naming internal systems, since this will be public.
